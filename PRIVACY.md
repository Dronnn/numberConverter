# Number Systems Lab privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy covers the Number Systems Lab iPhone and iPad app (`com.mrmaier.NumberConverter`).

## On-device data

Conversions, calculations, and answer checking run on your device. The app does not send your entered numbers or answers to the developer and does not save a conversion history. Your two’s-complement preference and aggregate quiz counters are saved in the app’s own local storage. The counters contain questions asked and answers correct, not the text of your answers. No account is required.

## Optional tips

Tips are optional, one-time App Store purchases. Apple processes payments and manages its purchase records. The app receives StoreKit product and transaction information to display prices and verify tips. Verified transaction identifiers are held in app memory to prevent duplicate acknowledgements and are not sent to a developer server. Every feature remains available without tipping. Apple’s handling of account and payment information is described in [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Diagnostics and tracking

The app has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or developer-hosted backend. Apple’s MetricKit framework may deliver diagnostic and performance information to the app. The app writes summary counts and measurements to the device’s system log, without the numbers or answers you enter. It does not upload these logs or payloads to the developer. Apple’s own analytics and diagnostics are governed by your device settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

## Optional contact

Contacting the developer from Help opens Apple’s mail composer. Nothing is sent until you choose Send, using your configured mail service. The app does not automatically attach your numbers, answers, quiz counters, or logs. Names, email addresses, and message content that you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable legal obligations. You can request its deletion through the same Help contact entry.

## Controls and retention

You can reset quiz counters in Statistics and change the two’s-complement preference in the converter. Other input is kept in memory while the app retains that screen state. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device; offloading and device backups may retain or restore data according to your Apple settings. Resetting or deleting the app does not erase Apple’s purchase records or messages already sent. Apple and your mail provider offer their own controls for those records.

## Questions and changes

For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact the developer through Help. Non-confidential questions can also be posted in the [project’s issue tracker](https://github.com/Dronnn/numberConverter/issues). Issues are public: do not include private numbers, personal information, or payment details. This policy will be updated when the app’s data handling changes; the date above identifies the latest version.
