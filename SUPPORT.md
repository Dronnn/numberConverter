# Number Systems Lab support

For help or a bug report, use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/Dronnn/numberConverter/issues). Reports are public: do not include personal information, private numbers, Apple Account details, receipts, or payment information. Include the app version, device, iOS version, language, and steps to reproduce the problem. A small, non-confidential example helps explain conversion problems.

## English

Number Systems Lab converts numbers between bases 2–36, calculates across bases, and provides quizzes and worked examples. Converter, calculator, quizzes, and built-in Help work offline. Optional tips use Apple's App Store and require a network connection.

- For bases above 10, use letters A–Z for digit values 10–35. Unsupported digits remain visible so you can correct them; the app explains the error.
- Use the question-mark button to open Help. It includes integer and fractional conversion examples and the privacy policy.
- Tips are optional, one-time consumable purchases. All features remain available without a tip; there is no subscription or feature unlock.
- The App Store determines the currency and current price for your storefront. If prices cannot load, you can retry or continue using the free tools.
- For an App Store charge or refund, use [Apple's purchase support](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). Do not post payment details in a public issue.
